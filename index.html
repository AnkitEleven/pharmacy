Pharma Anveshan Rajasthan – 2026

NIMS Institute of Pharmacy proudly organised Pharma Anveshan Rajasthan 2026 under a prestigious Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) grant. The landmark event gathered pharmacy council leadership, distinguished educators, industry leaders, and research scholars from across the country to discuss modern curriculum integration and future healthcare advancements.

APTI Education Excellence Award – 74th IPC Bengaluru

NIMS University received the celebrated APTI Education Excellence Award at the 74th Indian Pharmaceutical Congress (IPC) in Bengaluru, presented by stalwarts Dr. R.N. Gupta, Prof. Dr. B. Suresh, and Dr. Milind Umekar to Prof. Dr. Meena K. Yadav and Dr. Junaid Tantray in recognition of outstanding academic and research contributions.

National Formulary of India (7th Edition) – IPC Ghaziabad

Students and faculty of NIMS Institute of Pharmacy witnessed the historic release of the 7th Edition of the National Formulary of India (NFI 2026) at IPC Ghaziabad, graced by Shri J.P. Nadda Ji, Hon. Union Minister of Health & Family Welfare.
